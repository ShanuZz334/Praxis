12. Developer Notes
Every event card follows one schema, regardless of whether it's macro, corporate, geopolitical, or commodities. 
The UI is completely driven by the extracted JSON—no custom logic per category. 
Keep Reasoning, Keywords, Historical Similarity, and any future AI fields in the database, but hide them from the card. They power search, filtering, the decision engine, and future features without cluttering the interface. 
If extraction confidence is low, flag the event for review or reduce its influence on the Event Score. 
This blueprint gives you the same level of consistency that you've established for your indicator specifications: every event becomes a standardized, machine-readable object that can be displayed, scored, filtered, and integrated into the overall Praxis Decision Engine.
